1. Introduction

Online Atmosphere Studios respects the privacy of people playing Alien Atmosphere. This policy explains what information the game processes, why it is needed, how it is stored, and how players can request deletion of their saved information.

2. Information Processed

Alien Atmosphere communicates with an external game server to process gameplay and maintain saved progress.

  • Game identifiers: Locally generated identifiers and server-issued session identifiers used to associate gameplay with sessions and saves.
  • Gameplay information: Player actions, inventory, progress, world interactions and other information needed to operate and save the game.
  • Technical connection information: Network connections involve technical information, including IP addresses. The game does not intentionally use this information for advertising or profiling.

Players are not required to provide their name, email address or password to play the current version.

3. How Information Is Used

  • Operating the game and processing player actions.
  • Maintaining gameplay sessions.
  • Saving and restoring game progress.
  • Providing game-world features and interactions.
  • Maintaining service security and reliability.

4. Advertising and Analytics

The current version of Alien Atmosphere contains no third-party advertising or advertising-tracking systems. No third-party analytics SDK was identified in the implementation reviewed for this policy.

Gameplay information is not used for targeted advertising.

5. Data Storage and Security

Saved gameplay information is stored on a server hosted by DigitalOcean in London, United Kingdom.

The Android application is configured to communicate with the server using HTTPS, encrypting information transmitted between the game and its public endpoint.

The game also uses local caching to reduce unnecessary network communication.

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information, although no internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Third-Party Services

The game uses the following infrastructure services:

  • DigitalOcean: Game server hosting.
  • DuckDNS: Domain name resolution.
  • Google Play: Android application distribution.

These providers may process technical information according to their respective privacy policies. Online Atmosphere Studios does not sell player gameplay information to advertisers or data brokers.

7. Data Retention

Saved gameplay information is retained to allow players to resume their games. There is currently no stated automatic expiry period for saved games.

Players may request deletion of their server-side saved gameplay information by contacting the developer.

8. Data Deletion Requests

To request deletion of saved gameplay information, email hancockrobertjohn@gmail.com .

Please provide enough information to identify the relevant saved game. Do not send passwords or other sensitive credentials.

Additional information may be requested where necessary to identify the correct save and prevent accidental deletion of another player's information. Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable data protection requirements.

9. Children's Privacy

Alien Atmosphere is not specifically designed for children. Its intended audience and applicable age restrictions will be identified through its Google Play declarations.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when game functionality, data processing or supporting services change. The latest version will remain available at this address.
